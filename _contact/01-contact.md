Points of contact for communication about OpenPGP.

## General OpenPGP Contact

Implementation-agnostic exchange about OpenPGP, the standard.

### IETF OpenPGP working group

The [OpenPGP working group at the IETF](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/openpgp/about/) standardizes OpenPGP as a format, in the form of RFC documents.

An [email list archive](https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/browse/openpgp/) is available.

[Subscribe](https://www.ietf.org/mailman/listinfo/openpgp)

### openpgp-email (hosted by enigmail)

An email list for discussion of "OpenPGP-based Email Encryption":

<https://lists.hostpoint.ch/archives/list/openpgp-email@enigmail.net/latest>

Note: The ["email summit"](/community/#openpgp-email-summits) series of meetings originates from this list.

## Projects

Project specific discussion of OpenPGP - centered around individual OpenPGP libraries, or applications that use OpenPGP.

### FreePG

The [FreePG](https://freepg.org/) project uses an email list as its main medium of communication:

<https://openpgp.simplelists.com/freepg/>

### GnuPG

The [GnuPG](https://gnupg.org/) project uses email lists as its main medium of communication:

<https://lists.gnupg.org/>

### Hagrid (and keys.openpgp.org)

The [hagrid]([https://minipgp6.org/](https://gitlab.com/keys.openpgp.org/hagrid)) keyserver software (as used by the keys.openpgp.org service) uses a [Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_(protocol)) chat room as it main point of contact:

<https://matrix.to/#/#hagrid:stratum0.org> (`#hagrid:stratum0.org`)

This chat room is also available via a bridge from the OFTC IRC network, as `#hagrid`.

### minipgp6

The [minipgp6](https://minipgp6.org/) project uses a [Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_(protocol)) chat room as it main point of contact:

<https://matrix.to/#/#minipgp6:matrix.org> (`#minipgp6:matrix.org`)

### Sequoia-PGP

[Sequoia-PGP](https://sequoia-pgp.org/) uses an [IRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRC) chatroom as its main point of contact:

`#sequoia` on [OFTC](https://www.oftc.net/)

In addition, Sequoia-PGP operates email lists via <https://lists.sequoia-pgp.org/>.

### Thunderbird (OpenPGP)

The Thunderbird email software operates a [Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_(protocol)) chat room for communication about OpenPGP support in Thunderbird:

<https://matrix.to/#/#openpgp:mozilla.org> (`#openpgp:mozilla.org`)
